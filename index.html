L'EDITORIALE · TRE SETTIMANE DOPO

Tre settimane, una nuova geografia

Il campionato ha cambiato forma. Verdi FBC ha preso il controllo delle due classifiche, Fran resta incollato alla vetta e Crystal Free Palacetine ha messo il turbo con due settimane consecutive da oltre 83 punti. Dietro, Don Balon scopre quanto pesa difendere un titolo e la lotta Pizza si divide in due: Simone ultimo nel Generale, Dario ancora senza vittorie negli Scontri.

Three wins from three and control of both tables. Paolo isn't putting on a show, he's simply collecting points with the cold efficiency of someone who knows exactly how long a season is.

Don't Touch My Cozz · Fran

8 · MATURO · 2nd Generale · 2nd Scontri

Still unbeaten in the Scontri and only 5.5 points behind Verdi in the Generale. For years the interesting squad; right now there is considerably more substance than curiosity.

Crystal Free Palacetine · Marco Mac

8.5 · CRUISE CONTROL · 3rd Generale · 3rd Scontri

64.5, then 83, then 83.5. Palace have gone from drifting to third in both tables, with the 3-1 win over Don Balon proof that this is no sightseeing trip. For once Marco's route planning leads towards the summit — and the points are being redistributed very much in his favour.

WhySoSerious · Paolo S

7 · VIGILE · 4th Generale · 6th Scontri

Still fourth in the long table and managed to put Don Balon away 2-0. Not loud enough to dominate the conversation, not far enough away to ignore. Lurking.

FC MATTISIX · Matteo

6.5 · INCOMPIUTO · 5th Generale · 4th Scontri

After the glorious destruction of Don Balon came three consecutive draws. Still unbeaten in the Scontri, still competitive, but the great charge has temporarily become a committee meeting.

SS Blue Eagle · Dario

Don Balon · Marco Salvagnini

The defending champion has won once in five. The brief recovery against Fabs was followed by defeats to Paolo S and Marco Mac. Last year's swagger has become damage control.
