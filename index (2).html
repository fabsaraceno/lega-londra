L'Editoriale · Quattro Giornate Dopo

Fran takes the wheel. Paolo G kicks the door down.

Four weeks have turned the early hierarchy upside down. Don't Touch My Cozz has passed Verdi to lead both classifications, while Here We Don FC has gone from interesting newcomer to genuine menace. Behind them, Dario has produced some of the biggest scores of the season and still discovered new ways not to convert them into Scontri points.

The Back Page · La Svolta

COZZ YOU BELIEVE IT?

Fran is now top of both tables: 690.5 in the Generale and 18 Scontri points. Verdi remain level on 18 in the head-to-head table but trail on the tie-break, while Paolo G has stormed into third after a four-week run that included scores of 88 and 91. The title race has acquired new management.

690.5 Leader Generale (Cozz)
18 pts Trio di testa Scontri
95.0 Picco Dario (G6)

The defining result of the period came immediately: Fran beat Paolo P 3-1 in Giornata 6 and never looked back. Across the following rounds, Don't Touch My Cozz added two draws and another win before finally losing to Palace in Giornata 9. It was still enough to finish the block first in both competitions. Verdi recovered from back-to-back defeats, including a brutal 5-1 against Paolo G, to win the next two and stay attached to the summit.

Paolo G is the real gate-crasher. Here We Don FC scored 75.5, 88, 69 and 91 in the Generale over Giornate 6–9, won three of four Scontri and now sits third head-to-head and fourth overall. Meanwhile Dario's SS Blue Eagle scored a ridiculous 95 in Giornata 6 and 81 in Giornata 9, climbing to third in the Generale while somehow remaining tenth in the Scontri. Fantacalcio remains committed to absurdism.

Il Caso della Settimana

Shareholder confidence is holding up better than results

Don Balon managed only one Scontri win across these four rounds, losing to FantaOzzy and Verdi before another defeat in Giornata 9. The reigning champion is eighth in the Generale and ninth in the Scontri with five defeats from nine. Club sources insist Marco Salvagnini retains the full confidence of Don Balon shareholders.

BOARD UPDATE // DON BALON: "The board remains impressed by the manager's commitment, though expectations for the Generale remain high."
